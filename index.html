ABOUT

Jerome Potter is a Grammy-nominated music producer and composer based in Carmel Valley, CA. As half of the electronic duo DJDS, he spent 15 years recording, touring globally, and producing for artists including Kanye West, Khalid, and Burna Boy. Currently, he brings his contemporary sonic expertise to visual media, composing original music and sound design for global brands like Nike, Google, and Intel, while recently expanding into long-form documentary scoring.
